## Evidence vocabulary

**Observed:** a genuine supplied artifact supports the claim. **Software-tested:** a dated check passed within its stated scope. **Proposed:** a design or future test, not a delivered result. **Unverified:** evidence is missing. An illustration is not operating footage.

## Engineering deep dive

Follow the Windows bridge, image/depth/pose/map flow, then HTTP and storage boundaries. Local inference does not by itself secure a LAN service.

Every new result needs a revision, method, conditions, raw observations, failures and limits. Explain actual choices; never invent measurements to fill a table.

## Presentation

English technical prose; authentic hardware photographs; clearly labelled diagrams and conceptual walkthroughs. No empty showcase sections, fake badges or simulated data presented as physical measurements.
